TwoRun Privacy Policy

Updated November 26, 2026

TwoRun is developed by Samual Frazier. The game works offline and has no advertising, tracking, analytics services, or user accounts.

Information saved on your device

TwoRun saves the nickname you enter, completed scores and completion dates, and your sound and haptics preferences in the app’s storage on your device. A nickname is enough to play. Your real name is not needed.

This information is used to show your local leaderboard and remember your preferences. TwoRun does not send it to the developer, share it with other players, or sell it.

Storage and deletion

Your saved information stays on your device until the app’s data is removed. You can change your nickname and preferences on the home screen. To remove the saved profile and scores, delete the app using Delete App in your device settings. Offloading the app keeps its data.

Device backups may include TwoRun’s saved data according to your Apple backup settings. You control those backups through Apple’s settings. Restoring a backup may restore saved game data.

Permissions and services

TwoRun uses the device’s audio and haptic features for game feedback. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or location. It includes no third-party advertising or analytics software.

Support

If you email support@thatsfrazy.com, Samual Frazier receives your email address and the details you choose to send, and uses them only to answer your request. Share only what is needed to explain the problem.

If you choose to request support through GitHub Issues, your request and replies are public. GitHub handles account and service information under its privacy statement. Samual Frazier uses the information you choose to share to answer your request and resolve the issue. Share only what is needed to explain the problem.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@thatsfrazy.com.

Policy changes

This policy will be updated if TwoRun’s data practices change.
