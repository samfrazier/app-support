SnakeArena Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Game data

SnakeArena is an offline single-player game. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. The game does not send your gameplay, scores, or settings to us.

Your personal best and preferences are stored on your device. You can remove this data by deleting the app or clearing its data where your device offers that option. Your device’s backup and restore settings may apply.

Support

Information you submit through GitHub Issues is public and is used to respond to your request. Leave out personal information.

For privacy questions, see SnakeArena Support.
