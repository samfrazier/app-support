SnakeArena Support

Email support@thatsfrazy.com. Include your device model, operating system version, and what happened.

If you have a GitHub account, you can also report an issue on GitHub. GitHub reports are public, so leave out personal information.

How to play

Drag to steer. Hold Boost to use charge. The meter refills after you empty it. Collect food to grow, avoid rival snakes and the arena edge, and follow the gold compass to find the leader.

Saved scores

SnakeArena works offline and keeps your personal best and preferences on your device.
